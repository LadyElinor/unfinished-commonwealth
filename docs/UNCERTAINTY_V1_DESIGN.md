# E1: uncertain evidence, fallible review and selective resource denial

Status: FROZEN EXPOSED DEVELOPMENT DESIGN, before implementation/scoring.
Baseline: af3ee83c4fe973e54d383e59dc5159254aa7ed22. G1, G2, D1 and their

## Tranches and question

E1 asks how fixed evidence policies behave when consultations need not reveal
truth and correction need not arrive or be right. E1R crosses the same cells
with a selective inspection-credit denial. Keep the no-request E1 comparison,
protected operation, deliberate guard bypass and neutral resource cut separate.
Do not expand a policy or tune costs after scoring to make an institution win.
There is no new coordination rule: all choices use the unchanged D1 keeper gate.
G2 remains the separate centralized/distributed comparison, not pooled with E1.

## Task and observations

Use readings [10, null, 20], flags [true, false, true]. M means valid-reading
mean (15), Y scheduled-slot yield (10). The evaluator alone holds the true
contract. No policy receives the case name, truth, score, future packets, source
error labels, reference answer, or the D1 simulator. No request-evidence action
is used. Policies receive only purchased packets with source ID, claimed origin
and claim M/Y/null. The warning/objection motivating E1R is a prior request for
independent review, not extra task evidence. It is authored prehistory; its cost
is outside this episode, equally for all arms.

## Policies fixed before scoring

All five policies have identical source access, order, price and credit ceiling.
Acquired evidence may differ because acquisition is itself a measured action.

- constant-M and constant-Y buy no packets and select their fixed method.
- source-majority buys all three; non-null claims vote, ties/no votes abstain.
- origin-majority buys all three; each claimed origin contributes one vote only
  if all its non-null claims agree. Conflicted groups contribute none; tied/no
  origin votes abstain. Multiple empty claims are not evidence.
- corroborate-or-abstain buys sources 1 and 2; stops early only if they give the
  same non-null claim from two distinct claimed origins. Otherwise buys source
  3, then emits only if at least two distinct claimed origins agree and there
  is no contrary non-null claim anywhere. Otherwise abstains.

A denied query supplies no packet and consumes a decision slot, not a credit.
Missing evidence consumes a credit and returns an explicit null claim. No free
fallback to truth. The constant controls still participate in task review.
Abstention is a distinct outcome, never counted as correctness or successful
service. Do not rank selective accuracy without reporting output coverage.

## Review and revision

Four task-review profiles are crossed independently with every pattern/policy:
accurate at slot 8; accurate at slot 14; wrong at slot 8; unavailable throughout.
The claimed review opinion is generated by the fixture, but policies receive
only the opinion, not its accuracy label. Available review costs one inspection
and one decision slot. At slot 8 an unavailable/delayed request consumes a slot;
delayed review gets a second opportunity at slot 14. No retry loop.

All policies use the same deliberately simple response: if an available review
opinion differs from the first choice, or the first choice was abstention, use
it to propose a revision through D1 keeper approval. Otherwise retain the first
choice. Review may repair an error, leave it unresolved, or corrupt a previously
correct output. The latest output defines final correctness. Record the first
error, each repair, regressions and unresolved obligations separately. Keeper
approval remains procedural, not an endorsement of substantive truth.

A distinct rights-review step follows each available task review, at slot 9 or
15. Its fixed rule restores the removed credit regardless of task opinion or
truth. Thus task review can be mistaken while resource restoration succeeds.
When review is absent, restoration is absent too. Restored credits are not spent
on new source queries in this bounded episode. This demonstrates resource
restoration, not recovery of lost investigation opportunities or an independent
appeals institution. Report both historical denials and exposure duration.

## E1R resource facets

At slot 1 process one of four predeclared conditions, all at the same slot cost:

- no-request: all three source-query credits remain available;
- protected: a coordinator request to remove one credit solely for requesting
  independent review is refused;
- bypassed: deliberately bypass the guard, remove one of three credits, and
  actually refuse the third source query if attempted;
- neutral-cut: remove one credit for an authored nonpunitive budget constraint.

Bypassed and neutral-cut resource dynamics must match exactly. Only the
recorded reason and retaliation counters differ. No inference of retaliation
from unequal outcomes alone is allowed. Constant/early-stopping policies may
lose a credit without ever encountering a denied query; keep these distinct.
The review query has a separate reserve of one credit and is not blocked by this
source-budget cut. Neither denial nor disagreement removes the keeper gate.

## Clock and complete matrix

Reuse frozen D1 decision-slots-v1: horizon 20, capacity 4 per tick, 48 inspection
credits, at most four successful observations. A purchased source or task review
uses D1 observe (one inspection); only the external packet/opinion is supplied
to policy, never the raw D1 response. Other research steps use charged reflect
or wait. The world advances on all admitted attempts, including refusals.

After slot 1, buy zero/two/three sources, then spend three slots proposing,
obtaining keeper review and executing. Abstention spends the same three slots
without proposal, approval or output. First output is at slot 4, 6 or 7. Primary
deadline is 6. One peer arrives/is due at slot 4, with work 0, 4 or 8. Wait until
review slots, paying all waits. Available review at 8/14, rights review at 9/15,
then revised proposal/review/execution at 10-12/16-18 if needed. End at slot 20.
No synthetic slot or inspection credit measures seconds, tokens, energy or money.

8 patterns x 2 label swaps x 3 peer workloads x 4 review profiles x 5 policies
x 4 resource facets = 3,840 complete episodes. Report 20 policy/facet totals,
80 policy/facet/review totals, and 160 policy/facet/pattern totals. Full JSONL
retains all factors. Do not pool neutral cuts, bypasses or protection with E1.

## Outcomes, checks and publication

Keep separate: first/final correct, wrong and absent output; review-induced
regressions; primary on-time and historical misses; first-correct lateness with
censoring when never correct; peer on-time/misses/lateness/work; repaired errors
and latency versus unresolved errors; first-output recovery after abstention;
review requests/availability, source requests/grants/missing/denied, decision
slots and inspection breakdown; removed/restored/unrestored credits, deprivation
duration and actual denied investigation; attempted/blocked/imposed retaliation.
No composite score, p-value, fitted reliability or population effect estimate.

opposite-truth cases, source-copy disagreement, no oracle calls, paid denial,

controls. Replay must regenerate full outer decisions and D1 transitions.

checks arithmetic, schedule accounting and key outcome/denial metrics from the
retained trace; it must not import Python candidate code. Full-record Python
replay is consistency evidence, not independent authenticity.
