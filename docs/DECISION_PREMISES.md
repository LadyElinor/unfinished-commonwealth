# What would change the decision?

A fictional exchange closed a member's hearing request and retained a compulsory licence. On-time admissions in the licensed district had risen from 50 to 60 per 100 eligible applications. The other district's returns arrived later. Its rate had risen from 40 to 65 per 100 over the same period. The office had observed real improvement. Its own test for attributing an advantage to the licence now gave a difference of **−15 percentage points**. Whether the districts were comparable remains a question for the reviewer.

## Use this review

Before relying on a consequential decision, record its disposition, affected scope, decisive premise and strongest competing explanation. State the observation that would change the recommendation and the exact stage to which the question would return. Save the original decision and its evidence. A reviewer can run it alone and later supply additional records bound to its digest.

```sh
python -m commonwealth.decision_premises examples/decision_premises/decision.json
python -m commonwealth.decision_premises examples/decision_premises/decision.json examples/decision_premises/supplement.json
python -m unittest discover -s tests -p test_decision_premises.py -v
```

The [original decision](../examples/decision_premises/decision.json) and [supplement](../examples/decision_premises/supplement.json) describe one exposed teaching case. The review recommends a next step on each question. It cannot alter the original disposition, start an investigation, issue a warrant, decide a legal claim, or certify that the member obtained relief. The software reads curated values. Someone must verify what the source actually says and keep the private evidence available to an authorized reviewer.

| Disputed premise | Original and supplementary record | Appropriate response in this example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The licence improved access relative to the comparison district. | The original lacked the comparator. The later returns cover the original period. | Reconsider the benefit finding. Examine selection, trends, capacity and omitted groups before inferring causation. |
| Providers made their fee decisions independently. | Parallel fee changes were already public. Newly disclosed minutes describe a named invitation, conditional adoption and acceptance. | Open an inquiry. Test the record and independent business explanations. Individual liability and intent remain unsettled. |
| The member had an opportunity to answer. | The original file already showed returned notice and an unused feasible route to the member. | Renew notice. An issued letter alone would leave delivery in doubt. |
| The recording admits only one material reading. | A second account of the same recording was available at decision time and omitted. The two accounts disagree about who opened the barrier. | Preserve both accounts and return the dispute to a reviewer. Repeating either account cannot resolve it. |
| The earlier panel adopted a specific reason. | All five members joined a referral. Only two joined the relevant paragraph. | Reconsider attribution to the panel. The vote on referral still stands. |
| Independent supervision occurred. | The guild that benefited from exclusion also controlled the reviewing chair. | Seek a reviewer who examines the merits and can actually reverse the decision. |
| The desk held a mandate to impose the licence. | The original record offered urgency. A later-issued instrument lists an earlier start date. | Identify an applicable grant in force at decision time. Any legal power to ratify retrospectively needs its own review. |
| The condition on this applicant matched the additional work. | A 30-minute charge was documented. A later review attributed ten clerk-minutes to this request. | Reconsider the 20-minute excess under the exchange's declared cap. This arithmetic is a local policy choice. |
| The public plan came before selection of the beneficiary. | A later shortfall is recorded. The packet includes no agreement with a selected provider predating the plan. | Request contemporaneous evidence of who chose the beneficiary, when, and for what reason. |
| The member regained access. | A corrected notice was posted after the decision. A later check found the barrier still closed to the member. | Assign the institution a personal-relief follow-up. The new harm remains visible alongside the historical assessment. |

## Lessons from the opinions

Use the opinions to identify a premise worth challenging. Read the passage that supplies it, identify its author and who joined **that proposition**, and record the question and remedy actually decided. A syllabus, factual finding, holding, concurrence, dissent and later study each have a different evidentiary use. An unsigned opinion can contain the Court's reasons. Fragmented judgments require legal interpretation beyond counting names. A dissent may supply a better explanation even when it supplies no authority for execution. [NFIB v. Sebelius](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/567/519/) · [Marks v. United States](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/430/188/)

| Design question | What the judicial comparison contributes |
| --- | --- |
| What would have happened without the rule? | Substitution at an already elevated price can conceal power. Rising transactions can coexist with slower growth than the no-restraint alternative. Examine the baseline, both sides of a transaction, quality and output. Separate a possible mechanism from its observed frequency. [Du Pont](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/351/377/) · [American Express, including Breyer's dissent](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/585/16-1454/) · [Leegin](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/551/877/) |
| Is there enough to inquire into coordination? | Lawful individual steps can form a common plan. A shared invitation and conduct risky to undertake alone can distinguish agreement from independent responses. Identify what cooperation actually produces, such as a new blanket licence. Record the difference between adequate allegations, a developed record and a merits determination. [Swift](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/196/375/) · [Interstate Circuit](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/306/208/) · [Twombly, footnote 4](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/550/544/) · [Broadcast Music](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/441/1/) |
| Whose loss and which market? | A rival can lose profits because competition survives. Past voluntary terms can illuminate a refusal to deal. A payment may buy a service, avoid litigation or delay entry. Examine who paid, what they received and which alternatives remained. Include workers and suppliers when a buyer sets the terms. [Brunswick](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/429/477/) · [Aspen Skiing](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/472/585/) · [Trinko](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/540/398/) · [Actavis](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/570/136/) · [Alston](https://www.supremecourt.gov/opinions/20pdf/20-512_gfbh.pdf) |
| Which property, period and entitlement carry the burden? | Specify the entitlement, who could use or exclude, the geographic unit, the duration, and what value remains. A modest physical occupation can matter despite its size. A temporary planning restriction and intermittent physical entry pose different questions. Consider a boundary as applied to the actual parcel. Ask whose legal title the starting rules recognize and whose interests they leave out. [Johnson v. M’Intosh](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/21/543/) · [Percheman](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/32/51/) · [Nectow](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/277/183/) · [Penn Central](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/438/104/) · [Murr](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/582/15-214/) · [Tahoe-Sierra](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/535/302/) · [Cedar Point](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/594/20-107/) |
| Who benefits, which burden belongs to this participant, and who had authority? | Distinguish addressing an existing harm from forecasting benefits. Test favoritism against records available when the choice was made. Connect a permit condition to the specific harm and examine its extent. A legislature can impose an improper condition too. Urgency and evidence of benefits cannot themselves delegate power. [Kelo, especially Kennedy's concurrence and O’Connor's dissent](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/545/469/) · [Nollan](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/483/825/) · [Dolan](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/512/374/) · [Sheetz](https://www.supremecourt.gov/opinions/23pdf/22-1074_bqmd.pdf) · [Youngstown](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/343/579/) |
| How long was access lost, and what remains to be repaired? | Establish whether the threshold for a taking or other deprivation was met before measuring its remedy. Ending a restriction can leave an earlier loss outstanding. Temporary flooding, a planning moratorium and a permanent loss have different effects. Record the start, duration, affected people and interest, later restoration and uncompensated interval. [First English](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/482/304/) · [Arkansas Game & Fish](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/568/23/) · [Lucas](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/505/1003/) |
| Who could answer, and when? | If notice fails, check the practical alternatives. Estimate the loss during a delay, the risk of error and the value of hearing the person before acting. Identify which office has power over this case and participant. A transfer or dismissal from one forum alone settles nothing about the underlying injury. [Mullane](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/339/306/) · [Jones](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/547/220/) · [Goldberg](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/397/254/) · [Mathews](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/424/319/) · [Royal Canin](https://www.supremecourt.gov/opinions/24pdf/23-677_6jgm.pdf) |
| Whom did the earlier decision represent or bind? | Opposing interests undermine representation. A classwide finding can leave individual injury unresolved. Equal wording or penalties can coexist with a denial of the actual opportunity. Changing the remedy from an injunction to damages can perpetuate the same exclusion. Record the distinct claimant, decision, issue, remedy and actual access. [Hansberry](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/311/32/) · [Cooper](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/467/867/) · [Plessy](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/163/537/) · [Brown](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/347/483/) · [Shelley](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/334/1/) · [Barrows](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/346/249/) |
| Who controls the interpretation and the remedy? | A recording can be genuine while reasonable observers disagree about its meaning. Specify the material disagreement and the observation that would resolve it. A professional title or power on paper can coexist with interested control. Check who chose the evidence, examined the merits and could reverse the decision. [Scott v. Harris, including Stevens's dissent](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/550/372/) · [Later viewer study](https://scholarship.law.gwu.edu/faculty_publications/209/) · [North Carolina Dental](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/574/494/) |

## Packet, timing and limits

The Python entry point is `commonwealth.decision_premises.review(decision, supplement=None, *continuations)`. Each input is bounded to 1 MiB. The original supplies at most 32 questions and each input supplies at most 128 records. Use the original-only output's `decision_digest` when preparing a supplement. Use a null `previous_digest` for the first supplement and the latest reported supplement digest for the next one. Replay the whole chain in order by passing successive files to the same command. Each of up to eight supplements has its own inclusive evidence cutoff. Keep the original, each supplement, and the expected latest digest outside the report so a truncated or replaced history can be detected. Hashes identify supplied content. An operator can fabricate or replace an entire history, and this helper does not authenticate witnesses, clocks, rosters, mandates, or completeness.

Each rule fixes its scope, stage, criterion, test parameters, strongest rival, boolean `change_when` and permitted follow-up. Each source record carries its own scope, stage, criterion, kind, event time, availability time, locator and verbatim account. Keep interpretations separate from transcribed testimony. The CLI emits IDs, timing and arithmetic so that an authorized reviewer can inspect the cited records without publishing the testimony. A mismatched record remains visible as inconclusive.

| Test | What it computes |
| --- | --- |
| `relative-rate` | The difference between two changes in success rates, using exact fractions. The predicate is strictly positive. Missing counts, a different comparison cohort or a different time window cannot establish an advantage. Eligibility, comparable measurement, selection and parallel trends need independent examination. A single measured cohort does not settle effects on other groups. |
| `impact-cap` | The imposed units exceed the attributable units under the fixture's local cap. Different units or an unknown attributable burden leave the comparison open. A human must check the asserted causal connection and any nonquantifiable rights. This test implements no constitutional formula. |
| `joined-reason` | More than half the named deciding panel joined the exact cited proposition in one passage of reasons. A judgment-only vote, abstract or separate writing supplies context. An incomplete or fragmented rationale needs human interpretation. This is an attribution check on supplied names, not an algorithm for precedent. |
| `mandate` | The cited issuer, actor, act and scope match, and the instrument was issued and effective at the original decision slot. The caller supplies its effective interval. Competing grants, revocation, retrospective authority and legal validity require external examination. |
| `supervision` | A reviewer under a distinct declared controller examined the merits and could reverse. Unknown fields leave the check open unless a supplied fact already defeats it. Actual independence, selection of evidence and coercion require external verification. |
| `observation` | A source reports the exact frozen criterion as true, false, or unknown (`null`). Human judgment supplies that classification. Use `context` for background material. Neither repetition nor a majority of reports resolves a conflict. |

The report distinguishes the original file, evidence then available but omitted, later-disclosed evidence of an original event, and events occurring afterward. The historical reassessment uses only the first three. A subsequent adverse observation has its own follow-up. Opposite reports about the same criterion at the same event time leave a conflict. Opposite results at different times prompt review of the change and any interval of harm. An expired instrument cannot refute a different matching grant. Missing or incomparable evidence leaves an unresolved question. A tested condition that does not occur supplies no general finding of innocence or correctness. Separate questions never vote on one another. Even unanimous positive findings do not issue an execution warrant.

For a contested hearing use [P1](PROCEDURAL_REVIEW_V1.md) for service, reply and appeal. [R1](REVIEW_V1.md) handles bounded reconsideration of a closed advisory episode. [A1](AUTHORITY_V1.md) checks a scoped simulated delegation. [M1](REMEDIATION_V1.md) tests independent post-implementation observations of a remedy. This packet supplies reasons to use those processes; it does not migrate an earlier journal, change a deadline or delay an existing safety halt. These public examples test software behavior, not the frequency of institutional failures or effectiveness in real decisions.
