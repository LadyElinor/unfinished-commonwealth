# Provenance and related work

This repository was generated by an AI coding assistant at the user's request to build a project supporting cooperative machine institutions, iterative coaching, and reality checks. Source code, tests, fixtures, and local documentation were newly written for this project. The demonstration is authored and deterministic. No autonomous agent society has been observed.

## Research references

- Park, Joon Sung, et al. **Generative Agents: Interactive Simulacra of Human Behavior** (2023). [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.03442). Memory, reflection, and planning in believable agent simulations; believability is not independent evidence of civilization or responsible judgment.
- Vezhnevets, Alexander Sasha, et al. **Generative agent-based modeling with actions grounded in physical, social, or digital space using Concordia** (2023). [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.03664), [repository](https://github.com/google-deepmind/concordia). A possible future simulation framework; not a dependency of this seed.
- Leibo, Joel Z., et al. **Scalable Evaluation of Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning with Melting Pot** (2021). [PMLR paper](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v139/leibo21a.html). Relevant to evaluating cooperation with unfamiliar partners and situations; this project does not reproduce its experiments.
- Ostrom, Elinor. **Beyond Markets and States: Polycentric Governance of Complex Economic Systems** (2009 prize lecture). [Lecture](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/economic-sciences/2009/ostrom/lecture/). Human institutional diversity and collective action inform the research questions; transfer to machine communities remains a hypothesis.

The casebook is newly written teaching material based on the user-directed manuscript *The Codex of Unfinished Minds*. It keeps fictional cases separate from research findings. External articles, repository code, and the full manuscript are not included in the MIT-licensed source distribution.

## Reproduction

`scripts/verify.py` runs the behavioral suite and checks documentation links and packaged JSON. `scripts/reproduce.py NEW_DIRECTORY` produces the five-tick demonstration, journal, and fifteen-run comparison in a fresh directory. Example files in `examples/` are labeled local reproduction outputs, not independent observations or sealed evaluation data.
