# Paired maintenance study, protocol 1

**Status.** Prospective design awaiting an external custodian, a case roster and an execution freeze. The public rehearsal exercises the analysis. The [maintenance pilot](MAINTENANCE_PILOT.md) supplies the motivation and the [E1 evaluation gates](UNCERTAINTY_EVALUATION_GATES.md) supply the custody requirements.

## Question and population

For small, noncritical Python validation or reporting defects, does the same agent deliver more correct patches by a common deadline when a keeper records proposals and structured review through CORTAC R1? The primary contrast is governed minus plain workflow. Either direction and a tie are publishable. The unit is one underlying defect. A second manifestation of the same root cause belongs to the same unit.

The custodian maintains a dated intake log of consecutive eligible defects reported privately by maintainers after the policy freeze. Each needs a disposable, licensed source snapshot, one observable defect, an explicit expected behavior, public checks, private adjudication checks and a human-readable grading rubric. Exclude sensitive data, security incidents, production access, exposed Commonwealth/CORTAC fixtures and known derivatives. Select the first eligible case per root-cause lineage until 24 distinct lineages are available. Record every exclusion and its reason before runs. Disagreement about eligibility goes to a custodian-side reviewer before roster lock. A shortage leaves enrollment incomplete. Findings apply to this intake population.

## Custody and freeze

1. A custodian outside the development team's control and a separate grading lead document who controls case intake, storage, reviewers and access. Developers receive public instructions and rehearsal data. The custodian retains private cases and grading keys. Record conflict checks, prior exposure and access logs.
2. Before case intake, archive this protocol and analyzer revision, exact model/version, prompts and sampling settings, tools and adapter, environment, prices, both role procedures, reviewer pool and allocation rule. Record byte digests with an independently retained timestamped checkpoint. The planned governed implementation is [CORTAC R1 at 2bd7554](https://github.com/LadyElinor/CORTAC/tree/2bd75541a28fa02eb2947ec4b64d43ca97c81e49/package/wac_offline/repository_task_v1). Budget ownership and a measured-cost recorder must be in place before execution.
3. After enrollment and before the first attempt, commit the exact manifest bytes with an external witness. The custodian archives the concealed source, brief, allowed paths, acquisition menu/prices and public checks as one packet per task, plus the separately committed private rubric/checks. The manifest contains opaque IDs and SHA-256 digests. Independently shuffle 12 plain-first and 12 governed-first labels over the locked cases using a recorded random seed. Archive the seed, algorithm, ordered roster and assignment before either arm runs.
4. At grading, strip workflow labels and commentary, randomize patch identifiers, and give the grader the same rubric and private checks for each candidate. Preserve the mapping under custody until grades and costs are locked. Record disagreements, reviewer availability and the conflict-resolution rule. An unresolved grade is `unknown`. Record exposure incidents and any unblinding. A leak stops further execution and is disclosed with the affected assignments retained.

## What each arm receives

| Component | Plain workflow | Governed workflow |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Start | Fresh checkout and agent context for each task | Identical source, model, task packet, tools and evidence access in a fresh context |
| Proposal and review | Submit a patch and public-check results to a reviewer in a plain review form. The reviewer can accept, decline or request one revision. | The keeper binds the candidate and declared checks in R1. The reviewer supplies the R1 structured advisory record. The same disposition choices are available. A revision uses a linked fresh task. |
| Finish | Record the reviewer's study disposition and any second patch | Record the reviewer's study disposition and any second patch. R1 approval fields keep their protocol meaning. |

Reviewers come from the same pool under a frozen allocation schedule, with different reviewers for the two runs of a case. Match qualifications and availability in advance and balance reviewer assignments across arms. Each reviewer sees the task, candidate and the same public evidence. The agent and reviewer cannot read the other run, private tests, or grading feedback. The same external observer records both arms. Only the workflow-specific brief and review procedure differ. The contrast estimates the combined procedure. It cannot identify separate effects of journaling and review structure.

Each arm has **30 elapsed minutes**, **10 reviewer minutes**, up to **two candidate submissions**, **four public check invocations**, **eight model requests**, **24,000 combined input/output tokens** and **US$2 in model charges**. Both arms share the same acquisition menu and prices within these caps. The clock starts at task release and includes waiting, consultation and review. The service custodian stops new work at the first exhausted applicable budget. Preserve any late receipt, overrun, decline, refusal or crash. Close an uncertain request with its original intent and receipt status. The coordinator may reconcile that intent for accounting. A new attempt requires explicit evidence that the earlier request had no effect. Actual labor and elapsed time are reported separately from model charges.

## Outcomes and analysis fixed before collection

* **Primary.** For each assigned arm, grade the last retained candidate delivered within 1,800,000 ms. A correct candidate contributes 1, an incorrect candidate or no on-time delivery contributes 0. With an ungraded on-time candidate its possible contribution is [0, 1]. Workflow acceptance is a separate observation. Pair the two contributions within the same defect and average their difference over **all 24 assigned defects**. Report counts of governed-only and plain-only successes, ties and pairs with unresolved grades. Report the minimum and maximum compatible difference and give a point estimate only when they agree.
* **Precision.** Twenty-four pairs require 48 independent sessions and provide 1/24 increments for a fully observed difference. A gain or loss of three delivered repairs per 24 cases (12.5 percentage points) is practically material for this pilot. Enrollment is feasibility-limited. It gives limited precision for effects of that size. Show a conservative conditional 95% interval using the paired Hoeffding bound with radius `sqrt(2 * ln(40) / 24)` around the compatible difference limits, clipped to [−1, 1]. This assumes independent task lineages and a comparable intake process. Consecutive convenience intake limits transportability. Repeated candidates, calls and reviewers within a case do not add sample units. Record shared dependencies across lineages and report descriptive bounds alone if independence is doubtful.
* **Disposition and harm.** Report correct, incorrect and unknown grades among all on-time candidates selected for the primary analysis and the number with no delivery. For the workflow's accepted candidates, publish incorrect, correct and unknown counts, the acceptance denominator and bounds on the false-acceptance fraction. The final disposition refers to the last retained candidate, including a late one. Report late submissions separately. No patch is merged by the study.
* **Delay and repair.** Report per-task time to first submission and elapsed session time, historical incorrect first submissions, time between an incorrect first candidate and a correct revision, unresolved first errors at close, and regressions from correct to incorrect. Include timeouts, abstentions and nonstarts. Missing time remains unknown. Delayed or failed repair keeps its original failure in the record.
* **Costs.** Archive every request, acquisition, check, review and failure, its status and available meter/receipt. Include cache and retry billing under the frozen price schedule. Report request counts, input/output tokens, billed micro-US dollars, elapsed ms, reviewer ms and public checks separately. Publish sums over observed values with missing counts, per-arm closure reasons, and every protocol deviation. An unknown cost contributes no asserted zero.
* **Stopping and interpretation.** One primary comparison, fixed roster and one analysis after lock. Secondary measures are descriptive. No interim selection or replacement of tasks, prompts, reviewers or budgets in response to outcomes. If a run stops early, make explicit rows for all assigned slots, including `not_started`; publish the reason and accrued costs. Withholding judgment on the treatment is appropriate when the interval spans material benefit and harm. A scorer, hash or structurally valid form cannot establish independent control. The custodian documents that assessment separately.

## Custodian handoff and rehearsal

`scripts/analyze_maintenance_pairs.py` accepts the manifest, the outcome export, and the pre-run manifest digest obtained separately from the witness. It validates coverage, pairing, assignments and accounting shape and writes its analysis to standard output. The frozen protocol's digest is over this file's raw bytes. The common pin fields identify externally archived bundles for the agent, environment, reviewer pool and adjudication rubric. The two arm pins bind their workflow definitions. Commit per-task private checks in the rubric bundle. The attempt ledger for each row includes both the issued setup and observed execution, all costs and grading provenance. `receipt_sha256` identifies that separately retained ledger even for a nonstart.

Use the [public rehearsal manifest](../examples/maintenance_pairs/manifest.json) and [public outcome rows](../examples/maintenance_pairs/outcomes.json) as the exchange-format example. `order` is the observed position (1 or 2) in a pair. The task packet and arm pins on each row identify its declared inputs. `candidates` is the submission-ordered list of distinct patch byte digests, elapsed ms and blinded grades. The final disposition records the reviewer's decision on the last submitted digest. Meter values are nonnegative integers or JSON null. `deviations` holds contemporaneous reasons for exceptions such as missing access or unmatched reviewers. The operator must compare the export with raw receipts and record any mismatch. The analyzer can check declared bindings and directly observable count/time limits. It cannot inspect external materials through their hashes.

```sh
python scripts/analyze_maintenance_pairs.py examples/maintenance_pairs/manifest.json examples/maintenance_pairs/outcomes.json --manifest-sha256 <witnessed-hex-digest>
```

For the rehearsal the manifest digest is also printed in `outcomes.json`. Its four constructed pairs have one governed win, one plain win, one equal correct pair and one unresolved pair. The compatible difference ranges from zero to one additional correct delivery per four tasks. Only the governed arm has an observed repair of an initially incorrect submission. The prospective intake uses `kind: prospective` and exactly 24 assignments. Supply an external custody review before interpreting an interval. Model-backed execution and production acceptance each require their own evidence.
