# Governance procedures: source review and Commonwealth boundaries

Reviewed 2026-11-05. Official accounts describe prescribed institutions; they do
not establish actual compliance, causal effectiveness, or comparative superiority.
The project studies mechanisms while retaining individual autonomy, accountable
authority, protected dissent, due process, and correction without inferred guilt.
No source grants this project political jurisdiction or changes its mandate.

## Sources and scope

| Primary source | Relevant procedure or distinction |

| [CCP Constitution, amended October 2022](https://english.court.gov.cn/2022-10/27/c_824962.htm), Articles 10, 15–17 | Article 10, not 16, contains democratic centralism and the four subordinations. Article 16 requires lower organizations to implement upheld superior decisions and restricts public disagreement. Internal dissent has a bounded scope. |
| [Central Committee Work Regulations, September 2020](https://www.beijing.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengcefagui/202010/t20201013_2109438.html) | Defines the center's organizational authority; this is distinct from local administrative decision procedures. |

| [Resolution on Party Self-Governance, adopted October 29, 2026](https://en.theorychina.org.cn/c/2026-11-01/1555090.shtml), III | Specifies protection for differing views, prior evaluation, leaders speaking last and limits on personal substitution for collective decisions, alongside centralized Party leadership. | [Interim Regulations on Major Administrative Decision-Making Procedures, 2019](https://xzfg.moj.gov.cn/front/law/detail?LawID=443), Articles 2–3, 19–20, 25–30, 33–38 | Primarily covers county-level and higher local governments, with specified exclusions. Includes conflicting expert views, conflict exclusions, legality review, recorded dissent and reassessment. The chief decides after discussion and must explain departure from the majority; this is different from Article 17's Party voting rule. |

The 2019 rules also exclude the principal original assessment organization from
commissioned post-decision evaluation. Separation of named assessors remains
weaker than demonstrated independence of appointment, funding and evidence access.
The preceding passages are paraphrases; Chinese-language regulations are read
for the cited procedural scope, not offered as legal advice or certified translation.

## Decisions for this repository

- Preserve independent evidence access as a design objective. Retain filtered
  reporting only as an explicit failure control, with the original warning still
  visible to the evaluator. Never label an unreceived warning as answered.
- Separate receipt, a reasoned response, factual verification, authorization,
  execution and observed repair. Counting consultations is insufficient.
- Keep method selection separate from keeper approval. Neither majority support
  nor coordinator status enlarges a mandate. Existing A1/P1 boundaries remain.
- Preserve dissent and exonerating evidence, refusal, review and exit. A warning
  is a reason to check evidence, not a verdict of wrongdoing. Existing M1 keeps
  protective correction distinct from intent and punitive findings.
- Measure delays and service harms independently. Neither efficiency nor a
  composite utility score may silently override a rights constraint.
- Treat controller labels as declarations. The host can rewrite the experiment;
  replay and different agent names do not establish independent oversight.

The [G1 design](COORDINATION_V1_DESIGN.md) narrows implementation to a matched
decision/routing comparison using the existing D1 scheduler and keeper boundary.
Leader-speaking-order effects, enforceable protection against retaliation,
independent appointments and empirical political comparisons remain untested.
The legal and institutional systems described here are not being reproduced or
endorsed as a whole. Automated procedure checks cannot supply public consent,
legitimacy, authentic evidence or authority over nonconsenting people.
