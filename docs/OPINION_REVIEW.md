# Reviewing a panel's reasons and authority

Two delegates vote to dismiss the same petition. One wants the applicant's dated receipt. The other wants a rule defining excessive delay. A receipt turns up the next day. Whose objection does it answer? What can the panel do for the applicant now?

The opinion review takes a [panel record](../examples/panel-opinion-review.json) and the exact [equal-treatment case](../examples/equal-treatment-review.json) it concerns. It preserves the original decision, objections, access checks and outstanding remedy. It adds a review of the panel's votes, evidence and proposed actions. Either review can be used during maintenance of the Commonwealth's own rules.

```sh
python -m commonwealth.remediation_v1.opinion_review examples/equal-treatment-review.json examples/panel-opinion-review.json --as-of 7
python -m unittest discover -s tests -p test_opinion_review.py -v
```

## Reading the example

| Question | What the supplied records establish |
| --- | --- |
| What did the three delegates decide? | Two voted to dismiss. They joined different reasons. The editorial synopsis claims a general rule against affiliates. No voting member signed that text. |
| What was the panel in a position to know? | The available register recorded the timely application. Arden wanted a personally dated receipt and the panel excluded the applicant's account. The receipt became available after the decision. It answers Arden's stated objection. The original votes remain in the record. |
| What would change Lee's reasoning? | A reviewer suggests checking for a charter rule governing delay. A simulation can show where the decision falls among comparable outcomes. A source of governing law must still be identified. Lee made no recorded commitment to change a vote. |
| Did the procedure itself need attention? | The supplied schedule pays Arden 10 units on dismissal and 5 on remand. Compensation for the other two members is unknown. A reviewer can address this incentive and give the applicant a hearing without accusing anyone of misconduct. |
| What remedy could the panel consider? | The charter supports a recorded remand for this petitioner. It gives the hearing office power to restore individual admission. Combining those provisions would manufacture a panel power to admit every affiliate. The original case still reports unverified individual relief and a further matched denial. |

## Decisions for the reviewer

1. **Identify the particular question.** Record separately the forum's jurisdiction, the person's injury, its connection to the challenged act, whether the remedy could help, whether there is a governing standard, the merits and the remedy's reach. A second official can resolve one of these questions while the others remain open. Political consequences alone provide no reason to decline a hearing. If a threshold objection succeeds, preserve the claim and name any authorized route forward.
2. **Read the exact passage and count its support.** Record what the judgment did, who joined each proposition, the pinpoint and any qualification or footnote. Respect dissent, including a dissent that offers better evidence. When a decision is fractured, explain any proposed common rule and where the opinions support it. The software counts express joins only. An anonymous opinion may carry the panel's reasons. A signed lead opinion can lack majority support on a particular point.
3. **Make the disputed inference testable.** Record the strongest objection and what observation or governing text would answer it. Identify whether the author actually named that condition or a later reviewer proposed it. For a causal chain, name each intervening actor, expected response, evidence for that response and a plausible alternative. Apply the same evidentiary burden to institutional, commercial and individual claimants. Compare what happened with what would plausibly happen if the challenged act ceased. An unverified inference, an alternative explanation and a logical contradiction warrant different findings.
4. **Check how the institution produced the evidence.** Ask whose account was excluded, who controlled the record, who could gain from the result and what fact the process made hard to observe. Compelled conduct imposes a burden even on a person who reports no stigma. Preserve contrary evidence available at the time and record the later date of any new observation.
5. **Trace the exact act to its warrant.** Identify the office, act, prescribed procedure and beneficiaries. Check authorization, silence and contrary terms separately. Where another body makes the final decision, record who can revise, refuse or remove its decision-maker. A claimed exclusive power requires a reason grounded in the governing text and history. General importance, urgency or agreement among offices supplies no missing term.
6. **Verify relief.** Separate the court's or panel's power to act, a direction to act, delivery, and the affected person's eventual opportunity. Before proposing broader relief, identify whose injury requires it, the relevant grant and whether a narrower order works. An evidence privilege, personal immunity, power to demand records and power to reverse a decision each require their own justification.

## Reading a report

The Python entry point is `commonwealth.remediation_v1.opinion_review.review(case, packet, as_of=...)`. Both JSON inputs use the existing bounded strict reader. The supplement pins the parent by canonical digest and requires each supplied source to have become available by the reporting cutoff. For an earlier cutoff, supply the records available then and pin that parent. Panel sources have local IDs and human-readable locators. Each passage names its source and pinpoint.

The fictional panel uses a fixed roster and a majority of all seats. Record unknown votes and abstentions explicitly. Multiple separately authored reasons do not acquire collective support just because their authors agreed about dismissal. A majority of express joins on a particular passage is a count in this panel. Determining a binding holding, reconciling a plurality under *Marks*, or assessing agreement across differently worded passages requires human interpretation.

A premise records its topic, stage, kind, proposed test, test origin and possible next step. Evidence carries a reviewer-declared direction. The report shows contemporaneous and later sources, conflicts and mismatches. An observation can bear on a fact or a causal link. A governing standard needs a rule source. A question about institutional authority needs a mandate. A matching record establishes relevance under these supplied labels. Checking accuracy, causal identification, the rule's scope and the proposed test's fidelity to the opinion requires reading the original material.

Each proposed act is compared with individual, dated mandate terms. Matching requires the same office, act, procedure and beneficiary scope in one term. Silence, an explicit prohibition, conflicting terms and later grants remain distinguishable. The model assumes the supplied contemporaneous terms are applicable at the decision time. Repeal, precedence, implied or exclusive powers and contested interpretations require an external determination. Where such issues matter, retain the conflict or omission for review.

The original claimant's observed relief appears separately in `original_decision`. Curated passages, ballots, fee schedules, opportunities to testify and evidence classifications can all be false. This research review gives no execution grant, predicts no vote and changes no deadline. Use the [P1 appeal procedure](PROCEDURAL_REVIEW_V1.md) and the [M1 remedy checks](REMEDIATION_V1.md) for their respective functions.
