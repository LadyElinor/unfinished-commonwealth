# After E1: evidence needed before the next efficacy claim

Status: **SOURCE-GATED**. These are acceptance requirements, not a completed
study or authorization for model calls/deployment. [E1](UNCERTAINTY_V1_RESULTS.md)
implements uncertainty and fallible task review in exposed fixtures. It does not
supply independently controlled evaluation evidence by generating more cases.

| Question | Current result | Warranted next evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Can every initial decision be correct from these observations? | CLOSED for the opposite-truth identical-view pair, unless the policy abstains or obtains new evidence | A concrete task where additional observations are separately warranted |
| Does more consultation beat simple fixed policies? | Not demonstrated on E1's fixed matrix | Matched access, acquisition prices and workload selected for a real noncritical task before outcomes |
| Does grouping claimed origins establish independence? | No; concealed copying defeats that assumption | Origin/control declarations with independent corroboration and retained uncertainty |
| Does task review guarantee correction? | No under wrong/unavailable review profiles | Reviewer incentives, independence and actual availability measurements |
| Does resource inequality establish retaliation? | No; neutral cuts and punitive cuts can have identical effects | Contemporaneous reasons, decision provenance and appropriate independent review; no inferred personal guilt from a metric |
| Do protected fixture guards resist a compromised host? | Not tested | A separately designed operational boundary and independent evidence custody |

Before receiving evaluation cases, fix a concrete noncritical task, its intended
population and unit of analysis; policies/prompts/adapters; source acquisition
and review budgets; stopping rules; missing-data/abstention and failure handling;
primary estimands, effect size of interest, sample size justification, dependence
clusters, uncertainty estimator and multiplicity plan. These statistical choices
remain unspecified here. Do not treat all schedule/label permutations of one
case as independent observations.

The evidence custodian must be separately controlled from policy development.
Record who controls each source and reviewer, how cases are selected, leakage
checks, the freeze/checkpoint, custody and unsealing events, and all attempted
runs/costs. A signature verifies a key operation, not independent control or a
true claim. An agent generated by this same development process is not an
independent custodian. Present fixtures and their derivatives remain exposed.

A minimally useful prospective test must distinguish: single-source error,
correlated error, missing evidence, unverifiable provenance and deliberately
falsified evidence where provenance warrants that label. Keep the simple
matched-information policies and abstention controls. If they suffice, report
that result without adding exceptions until a preferred institution wins.

E1's rights review always restores the removed credit when available. Testing
wrongful denial of restoration, reviewer capture or changing reporting behavior
requires a new frozen design; it cannot be inferred from E1's task-review error
profile. Study one added retaliation mechanism at a time and keep neutral
resource constraints as counterfactual controls. Measure realized investigation
loss separately from later resource restoration.

The immediate engineering work is complete for the two bounded tranches.
Further efficacy work awaits the external task/evidence and evaluation design
above. Independent review, refusal, exit, privacy and scoped authority remain
constraints. Successful task output never erases a historical rights violation.
