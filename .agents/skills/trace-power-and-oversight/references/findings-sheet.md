**Oversight finding:** name the check and what happened to it.

**Conduct finding:** attribute only the supported act, knowledge and purpose.

**Legal disposition, if any:** specify party, decision-maker, standard, date and later developments.

**Consequences:** observed outcome and remedy; separate what a reviewer could have changed from what the reviewer would have changed.

**Next record:** custodian, date range, result that would strengthen the finding, result that would weaken it.

For Relay Atlas, encode each established action as a directed, dated, source-linked relationship. Keep “a witness alleged X” separate from “X occurred.” For a CORTAC replay distinguish order, acceptance, attempt, observation, audit and evidence of effectiveness. For Babalon, four investigators returning the same underlying passage still supply only one evidentiary origin.
