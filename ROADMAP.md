# Roadmap

## A1 authority and language boundaries: implemented separately

[A1](docs/AUTHORITY_V1.md) adds read-only review-packet input compatibility,
curated three-register comparison, synthetic scoped delegation, fresh review
after amendment/challenge, and separately budgeted correction with reserved
halt/revoke slots. [Executed failures](docs/AUTHORITY_V1_RESULTS.md) retain false
host declarations, unrepresented affected people and missing remedy authority.
The baseline implementations and fixtures remain frozen.

Before any operational adapter: establish authenticated issuers and identities,
protected enforcement outside the participant, externally validated mandates,
independent representation and review, and observed real effects/remedies. Fix
the evaluation protocol and obtain independently authored cases before claiming
improved decisions. More persuasive rhetoric or a completed packet is not an
acceptance criterion for delegated power.

## 0.1: persistent workshop, implemented

Bounded executable data work, peer consequences, observed predictions, testimony, coaching records, scoped lessons, succession, replay, CLI, and exposed scripted comparisons. The acceptance criterion is a reproducible correction and handoff without erasing the original cost. No model intelligence or institution-level efficacy claim follows.

## Separate study harness 1.0.0: deterministic portion implemented

The [D1 implementation design](docs/STUDY_V1_DESIGN.md) implements the bounded
part of [NEXT_STUDY](docs/NEXT_STUDY.md) separately from the existing pipeline
and historical reducers. Contrasting contracts, constant and selector controls,
matched evidence ceilings, decision-slot scheduling and preserved refusal
records are exercised in 432 exposed deterministic runs. The simple evidence
selector matches the participant; inherited advice and coaching show no
correctness advantage and can add delay. See [results](docs/STUDY_V1_RESULTS.md).
Release 0.1.3 remains the frozen workshop baseline, not a new package release.

The separate [P1.1 procedural extension](docs/PROCEDURAL_REVIEW_V1.md) now tests
notice, objections, reasoned dispositions, amendment, appeal, prospective stays
and append-only corrections. It has its own version and eight regression traces;
the D1 sample, budgets and results are unchanged. Review costs and procedurally
valid failures remain visible. Actual identity isolation and independently
operated reviewers remain unimplemented.
Renewed notice for new material and a bounded combined appeal preserve each
party's grounds and reply opportunity without inventing extra execution powers.
The original P1 replay rules and all historical records remain available.

The separate [F1 discernment study](docs/FRAUD_DISCERNMENT_V1.md) adds 150 exposed
advisory runs on documented fraud mechanisms and benign counterparts. It checks
scoped verification, independent origin, authority and evidentiary status.
Compromised trusted sources, false alarms, abstention and deadline misses remain
recorded. Real identity verification, free-text extraction and externally held-out
scam evaluation remain unimplemented. See [results](docs/FRAUD_DISCERNMENT_V1_RESULTS.md).

The separate [I1 integrity extension](docs/INTEGRITY_V1.md) tests commitment
attribution, disclosed departures, declared issuer/dependency quarantine and
exact-scope checks after prior success. Its 112 exposed runs retain undetected
compromise and the cost of blocking legitimate requests. F1 remains frozen;
I1 adds an optional advisory policy, not an operational firewall. Authenticated
origin discovery, independent recovery and real effect-authority invalidation
remain future work.

The separate [R1 review extension](docs/REVIEW_V1.md) records disclosure of contrary
material, reviewer conflicts/capacity, proportional service impacts, alternatives
and one bounded reconsideration tier. Its 14 exposed traces preserve the original
decision and costs. R1 advice cannot execute a restriction or clear I1 quarantine;
authentic evidence, substantive fairness and live recovery remain unproven.

## Later 0.2: model-backed participation

- Independently construct evaluation cases after policies and analysis are fixed. The original workshop's quality-stream fixtures and all study-v1 development cases are already exposed.
- Validate any timing model against the intended task. Study-v1 charges synthetic decision slots; the original workshop still advances only on observation. Neither measures real model latency.
- Pin provider, model, prompt, adapter, sampling settings, and resource budgets for each run.
- Run externally supplied proposal-only agents with a host-owned interface that cannot read keeper credentials, evaluation references, or other participants' private context.
- Record transport failures and unknown outcomes explicitly. Never retry an uncertain external effect blindly.
- Compare the same model with matched information and budgets: single planner, role-separated team, coaching intervention, and coaching plus casebook. Separate narrative exposure from extra evidence.
- Freeze the evaluation protocol before collecting scored results. Obtain withheld cases from a separately controlled process. Count all attempts and costs.
- Replace scripted accounts with consenting human participants or independently operated services where the study requires them; distinguish operator independence from correlated model weights.

Exit evidence: working adapters, boundary tests, provenance, actual model-call receipts, analysis of failed trials, and a report that can be independently reproduced. Neither the original fifteen scripted comparisons nor the 432 exposed study-v1 runs can satisfy this gate.

## 0.3: useful communal work and longer succession

Maintain a consenting, noncritical software collection with scoped work grants. Study obligations surviving model replacement, unreliable memory, unavailable reviewers, limited budgets, and changed data contracts. Extend the seed's keeper-controlled lesson review, suspension, and retirement into separately governed amendment and appeals with independently operated participants.

Add safe-export policies, retention/deletion handling, observer diversity, and real identity/credential isolation before storing personal accounts. Preserve appeals and access to evidence during disputes. Provide bounded standing permissions for routine work; urgent conditions do not create new authority.

## Later: federation as a research question

Multiple separately operated workshops could exchange evidence and practices while retaining their own policies. Verify operator control, resource accounting, and trust roots instead of counting identities. Study refusal, exit, unequal resources, legitimate amendment, and harms to nonparticipants.

Calling such arrangements a civilization would require an explicit account of the word and evidence far beyond convincing dialogue. Personhood, consciousness, moral standing, and institutional legitimacy are not settled by any release checklist in this repository.
